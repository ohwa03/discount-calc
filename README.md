# discount-calc
Discount Calc — Vital Air Mechanical
A quick shopping discount calculator. Enter an item's original price and the % off, and it instantly shows what you actually pay — with tax, deal alerts, and currency conversion.
Live app: https://ohwa03.github.io/discount-calc
---
What it does
% Off — type the original price and percent off, see what you pay
Tax built in — defaults to Alberta 5%, with a dropdown for every province/territory
Stacked discounts — handles "30% off, then an extra 15%" and shows the real combined %
Deal judge — the total glows green for a good deal, red if it's below your threshold (default 50% off, adjustable)
Find % — know the original and sale price? Get the actual % off
Currency converter — convert any amount between CAD, USD, and PHP (works backwards from any currency); also shows your final price in all three
---
Install on iPhone / iPad (iOS)
Open Safari (must be Safari, not Chrome) and go to:
https://ohwa03.github.io/discount-calc
Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen)
Scroll down the share menu and tap Add to Home Screen
Tap Add in the top-right corner
The Discount Calc icon is now on your home screen — tap it any time, works like a normal app
---
Install on Android
Open Chrome and go to:
https://ohwa03.github.io/discount-calc
Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner
Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app if it appears)
Tap Add / Install to confirm
The icon is now on your home screen — tap it any time
---
Notes
Works offline for all the discount and tax math once it's added to your home screen.
The currency rates need internet to refresh to live values. Offline, it uses an approximate saved rate (good enough for a ballpark). Tap ↻ refresh when you have signal for the exact current rate.
No login, no ads, no tracking — it's just a calculator.
